Fursuit care and reviewing are important to me. When I get a fursuit, I would like it to be a good transaction so I can share a postive review about it for everyone to see. Only fursuits made for or retrofitted for me will show up here.

Fursuit Care

Bissell LittleGreen, 95% water, 5% woolite* or free/clear detergent solution.

*there is debate as to whether woolite should be used in fursuit cleaning or not. with extra water-only rinses, i've found success in using woolite. the use of a free clear detergent is recommended by more makers, but I have had mixed results using it in spot cleaning with the bissell. better results with free clear when used in tub deep cleaning.
