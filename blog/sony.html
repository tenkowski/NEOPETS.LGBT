I don't care if you own a newer PlayStation unless you're playing a Hideo Kojima game on it.

I do have an issue with Sony's online security controls and practices, more specifically, their PlayStation Network (PSN).

For a company that has time and time and time and time and time again been hacked and denial of serviced, I refuse to do any business with PSN. They have no business holding onto any of my information if they can’t keep their service together. This also goes for any PC game that might require PSN (good on Sony for removing PSN from Helldivers 2 PC)

If had a newer PlayStation, I would make sure to play only my physical games or someone else's digital games offline as much as possible. If PSN multiplayer was still free, as it was in the PS3-era, I would not mind using it, as I would be able to freely create as many anonymous accounts as I wanted and still enjoy multiplayer. But Sony wants you to pay for multiplayer, and have one PSN with all your games and all your info on it. So the hackers can have a field day with it next time they get breached.

You’ve heard it before, but it’s true. PC is better.
