caruso used to be bluer. it was in their pawpads, scruff, sclera, highlights, and more. although the simulation they used to live in was not supposed to alter their appearance, the countless number of times their simulaton rebooted took a toll on the color in their body. remnants of their blueness can be seen in their eyes and desaturated neckerchief.

art by aisuart. cat tenko also got a badge like this in 2018. in fact, all art on this page is from 2018. caruso first learned that other tenkos existed and that they were not the true tenko in 2018, and sought to claim the title of being that tenko.

and so, by some curse, wish gone wrong, or other fuckup, caruso's world was turned into a simulation. in this simulaton, time was stuck in a loop and caruso was regularly brainwashed, their memories not left intact as the simulation constantly reset trying to calculate what caruso needed to do in the year 2018 to become the true tenko. the simulation was left running and abandoned for years.

tenko bori eventually plucked caruso and his world of this simulation so that they could live their lives normally again.

caruso really wants to be called tenko on the site, but that would cause so much confusion. as a result, we're naming this variant "caruso", the second best name you could give a variant of Tenko besides Tenko!
