caruso used to be bluer. it was in their pawpads, scruff, sclera, hair highlights, and more. the simulation they were forcibly put into was not supposed to have any ill effects on its inhabitant's bodies, yet the constant rebooting of the simulation gradually chipped away at caruso's blueness due to data rot. the simulation would always reset while they were asleep, so their blue eyes were shielded from all the "noise" their body had absorbed while the simulation was rebooting. that is why only their eyes and desaturated neckerchief remained blue

art by aisuart. cat tenko also got a badge like this in 2018. in fact, all art on this page is from 2018. caruso first learned that other tenkos existed and that they were not the true tenko in 2018, and sought to claim the title of being that tenko. yes, caruso was quite impulsive once, despite how relaxed you may view them in the art so far.

and so, by some curse, wish gone wrong, or other fuckup, caruso was put into a simulation. in this simulaton, time was stuck in a loop and caruso was regularly brainwashed, memories wiped as the simulation rebooted to recalculate the right timeline where caruso usurped tenko. the simulation was abandoned but continuted to run for years.

tenko bori eventually plucked caruso out of this simulation so that they could live their life normally again. caruso is now carefree, relaxed, and the mellowest version of tenko.

caruso really wants to be called tenko, but that would cause so much confusion. we have tenko bori and cat tenko at home! as a result, this variant has been affectionately named "caruso", the second best nickname you could give a variant of tenko next to tenko!
