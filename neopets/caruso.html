caruso used to be bluer. it was in their pawpads, scruff, sclera, hair highlights, etc. the simulation they were forcibly put into was not supposed to have any ill effects on them, yet the constant rebooting gradually chipped away at their blueness. each reboot put a heavy strain on the disk i/o, multiply that by the innumerable amount of times it was rebooted, and caruso was subject to a lot of data rot. one part of caruso's body was less affected by the data rot however: their eyes, which remained a beautfiul blue. it is theorized that the simulation was set to only reset when caruso was asleep. therefore, as caruso's body was "waterlogged" by the simulation scraping away at their bytes during a reboot, their eyes were faintly shielded from data rot, preventing them from losing all their color like the rest of their body.

caruso's neckerchief was a vivid blue color before they intentionally faded its colors. this is to remind caruso and others of the pride they have in their bittersweet backstory and appearance.

and so, by some curse, wish gone wrong, or other fuckup, caruso was put into a simulation. in this simulaton, time was stuck in a loop and caruso was regularly brainwashed, memories wiped as the simulation rebooted to recalculate the right timeline where caruso usurped tenko. the simulation was abandoned but continuted to run for years.

tenko bori eventually plucked caruso out of this simulation so that they could live their life normally again. caruso is now carefree, relaxed, and the mellowest version of tenko.

caruso really wants to be called tenko, but that would cause so much confusion. we have tenko bori and cat tenko at home! as a result, this variant has been affectionately named "caruso", the second best nickname you could give a variant of tenko next to tenko!
