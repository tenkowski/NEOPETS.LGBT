Story

Veirs never views a person or task superficially, they will always stray from the job description. You ask Veirs if the clothes in your cart are a good look on you, Veirs will judge your look, your intentions, your preferences and always give you sass and what you want.

Being a low paying interior guard for Jazan didn't mean Veirs never cared for the job, it afforded them precious time to see everything they wanted to see inside the palace, including all the swindlers and bandits that were brought forth against Jazan's throne, including Tenko.

When Tenko met Veirs at the throne, Veirs intervened and spared them from prosecution. The two had never met each other, but they were both alike in several ways and both looking for connections in a new world they both found themselves in.

Etymology

Veirsiru - "Vying Steel"

1. Veirs: Vying
2. Asiru: Steel. From Asiru kallpa in Quechua, which translates to "stainless steel object".
