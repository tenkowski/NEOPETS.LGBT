It's a hot summer day in the base and the team is sunbathing and playing games beside the guild pool. If you are today's visitor, by now someone has probably already told you to mind the mess in that one corner outside of the pool, it's supposed to look like shit.

There are a lot of holes in the ground over here, and you would think the team's base has had an Symol infestation, but you would be wrong, as you would soon find out if you stood there longer.

You gaze at the dugged up holes until you look over your shoulder to see Lasak grinning and prodding at you to invite you to some drinks and water sports. You take your leave from this side of the poolside.

The guild invited you over again, but you came pretty late, and it's way past sunset now. You were held up by stressful work earlier that day, and everyone has already vacated the pool. Nothing's going on there tonight, but that's what you think. You had time to talk to Tenko while you were gone and he told you to come to the corner since you brought it up in your texts before. You walk in a loop around the dimly-lit poolside with Tenko around and he ends up telling you that he and another Neopet make holes there all the time. "Why?"

"Well, have you ever seen a Mutant Tuskaninny before?", Tenko would ask you with genuine curiosity. You've seen Mutant Neopets before, but have forgotten what one of the Tuskaninny variety looks like. "Yeah, they are a little rare." Tenko would point his index fingers at the tip of his fangs, and draw a straight imaginary line downwards to give you a hint.

Ah! You remember what they look like now. But you still tilt your head and swivel around confusedly as you ponder what it all means. Meanwhile from the corner of your eyes, you see Taji, the guild's maid, coming outside to tidy up the area.

Tenko looks at her, then back at you with a sly smile. With his index fingers still pointing out, he bends his elbows backwards, lifts his chin up, and draws imaginary lines inwards towards his sides, implying that he lets Roku sink her teeth into the ground next to him while he's lying back in the middle, getting squished by her.

While his fingers are pointed behind him, he feels the delicate fabric of Taji dress rub against him, and he curls up his fingers as to not rip the fabric while he pets her back. "Hello!" Taji, resting her chin on top of Tenko's head, asks you both, "Are you two talking about Roku?"

She's laying on top of you, wiggling up your body to meet you face to face, left tusk in the dirt when she shifts her weight left, right tusk buried when she shifts right, both tusks sunk into the ground when she's finally pinned you down where she wants you. And you can't roll away either, you are boxed in by the tusks and hundreds of lbs of Neopet laying on top of you. You are also a feral Neopet, and the weight lovingly suffocates you!

You can never wipe those lidded eyes and closed smile off her face.

Roku does not travel with the team on mission critical expeditions, Roku's extra-long tusks and Tenko's love for having all her weight smushed on top of him are generally a messy combo, for her tusks and Tenko's back spikes sink into the terrain while they get their fix. And if not the lawn, less destructive alternatives include a good pressing by the edge of the pool or on pool float during a pool party with Lasak.

So about those tusks in the ground, first of all, why? She's about as spontaneous as Tenko and the lawn always seems to be the place they lock eyes with each other around the house, so it's only natural! And more importantly, she doesn't mind the taste of dirt in her teefers. Her Transmogrification into a Mutant Neopet has warped her sense of taste, and a lot of unusual things taste average at best to her tongue. It's no wonder no food goes to waste around the house, she'll lick anything clean and won't complain about it. She's the designated taste taster of the house, so to speak. Despite her appearance and antics, she's actually very well mannered, a very good Neopet for Tenko and the gang.
