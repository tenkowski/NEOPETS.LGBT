It's a hot summer day at the base and the guild is sunbathing and playing watersports in their private pool. You are luckily friends with at least two members of the guild, and you have no plans today, so congratulations, you are today's guild visitor of the day! (we don't get that many visitors) By now someone has probably already told you to mind the mess in that one corner outside of the pool, it's supposed to look like shit. You think the team's base has an Symol infestation, but you would be wrong as you would soon find out the more you look into it.

Of course, curiosity gets the best of you, and every time you're chatting with a guildmate, you can't help but see those big holes from the corner of your vision. When you are alone for a moment, you begin to investigate the holes more up close, when suddenly, you find Lasak grinning and prodding at you from behind, inviting you to some free drinks and a pool party. You can't refuse pool fun time with Lasak, and you leave the cluttered side of the pool to go be with her.

Days pass and the guild has invited you over again, whoopie! But you came pretty late, and it's way past sunset now. You were held up by stressful work earlier that day, and everyone has already vacated the pool. Nothing's going on there tonight, but that's what you think. You had time to talk to Tenko while you were gone and he told you to come to the corner since you brought it up in your texts before. You walk in a circle around the dimly-lit poolside alongside Tenko, and the topic gets brought up again. He admits to you it's not Symols, he and another Neopet intentionally tear this side of the pool up. You let out a slightly loud "why?!"

"Heh! Well, have you ever seen a Mutant Tuskaninny before?", Tenko would genuinely ask you. You've seen Mutant Neopets before, but have forgotten what a Mutant-flavored Tuskaninny looks like? You shake your head from side to side.

"Yeah, to be fair they are one of the more uncommon Mutants. Let me give yoy a hint." Tenko touched the tip of his sharp fangs and drew a straight imaginary line downwards. Your eyes lit up as you recalled what they look like.

"Ah! You remember what they look like now. But you're still tilting your head trying to figure out what this all means huh?" From the corner of your eyes, you notice Taji, the guild's maid, slowly approaching you two outside as she is tidying up the area.

Tenko looks at her, then back at you with a devious smile. He points his index fingers at his fangs again, this time doing a jump backwards while also drawing the imaginary line towards him with his fingers, implying he is pushed back while fangs sink toward him.

When he jumps back, he lands right into Taji's warm, welcoming body. The scent and softness of her outfit quickly envelop and relax down Tenko. Her delicate dress ever so slightly bobs back and forth from the weak wind in the background. "Hello!" Taji said while resting her chin against Tenko. "Are you two talking about Roku?" *she copies Tenko by drawing more imaginary lines from her fangs downwards*

"She's the reason Tenko pays me extra!" Pfff, you can imagine how much work she has to do cleaning around the house.

*Tenko and Taji go off-topic and flirt with each other for a bit before the conversation gets back on track*
